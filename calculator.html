Read the model before reading the number

This calculator treats the selected amount as total taxable income, not gross Kalshi deposits, contract notional, account balance, or necessarily the P&L shown by Kalshi. It applies the 2026 federal single-filer ordinary rate table and the 2025 California Schedule X rate table (single or married/RDP filing separately).

Included

  • Federal ordinary-income brackets for tax year 2026.
  • California ordinary brackets from the official 2025 Schedule X.
  • Amounts from $10,000 through $200,000 in $10,000 steps.
  • Progressive rates: the next step can cross a bracket.

Not included

  • Deductions, credits, dependents, or other income.
  • NIIT, AMT, self-employment tax, penalties, or withholding.
  • Capital-loss, wagering-loss, long-term capital-gain, or section 1256 rules.
  • A conclusion about the tax character of a particular Kalshi contract.

Why the California year matters: the research record does not contain a final 2026 California individual rate schedule. The 2026 FTB Form 540-ES instructions tell filers to use the 2025 tax table in that estimated-tax worksheet. This page therefore labels the California source as 2025 instead of silently calling it 2026.

Why income is included

Section 61 is the baseline inclusion rule. It does not settle the character of Kalshi event-contract profit.

Read 26 U.S.C. § 61

Do not use the combined number as a filing result. The actual amount depends on filing status, all other income, deductions, credits, contract character, losses, and the tax year. The calculator does not decide the open Kalshi character question and does not calculate penalties or withholding.
