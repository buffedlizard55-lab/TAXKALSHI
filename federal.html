Federal tax on Kalshi earnings starts with section 61. It does not start with a platform form, and it does not start with a 60/40 assumption.

Inclusion

Except as otherwise provided in this subtitle, gross income means all income from whatever source derived, including (but not limited to) the following items: … (3) Gains derived from dealings in property; (4) Interest;

26 U.S.C. § 61(a), retrieved 24 September 2026

Contract profit and interest are listed separately. A referral credit is still income if no exclusion applies, even though it is not in the illustrative list. The list is “including but not limited to.”

If the contract is a capital asset — consequences only

This section states the consequences Topic 409 gives for capital assets. It does not find that an event contract is one. Section 1221 was not line-read in this pass. That gap is on the method page.

  • Held more than one year before disposition: long-term. Held one year or less: short-term. Count from the day after acquisition through the day of disposition. Topic 409.
  • Net short-term capital gain is taxed as ordinary income at graduated rates.
  • Net long-term capital gain can be taxed at 0, 15, or 20 percent, depending on taxable income. Other capital-gain rates (collectibles, unrecaptured section 1250, section 1202) were not matched to these contracts and are not applied here.
  • Net capital loss offsets ordinary income only up to $3,000 ($1,500 if married filing separately). The rest carries forward. Topic 409.
  • Report on Form 8949 and Schedule D if this is the character used.

Section 1256 — the test, not the slogan

any gain or loss with respect to a section 1256 contract shall be treated as— (A) short-term capital gain or loss, to the extent of 40 percent of such gain or loss, and (B) long-term capital gain or loss, to the extent of 60 percent of such gain or loss

26 U.S.C. § 1256(a)(3)

The term “section 1256 contract” shall not include— … (B) any interest rate swap, currency swap, basis swap, interest rate cap, interest rate floor, commodity swap, equity swap, equity index swap, credit default swap, or similar agreement.

26 U.S.C. § 1256(b)(2)

Year-end mark-to-market is in section 1256(a)(1): a section 1256 contract held at year-end is treated as sold for fair market value on the last business day of the year. That rule is harsh if the contract later expires worthless. It is also a reason not to elect the label casually.

Qualified board or exchange, as the IRS quoted it in Revenue Ruling 2007-26, includes “a domestic board of trade designated as a contract market by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission.” KalshiEX received that designation on 4 November 2020. CFTC Release 8302-20. Designation is not the whole regulated-futures test. The mark-to-market deposit element of section 1256(g)(1) still has to be met, and the swap exclusion still has to be cleared. See the flag on how far this pass quoted subsection (g)(1).

Kalshi Klear’s 26 March 2026 filing says the event contracts it clears are swaps, are fully collateralized at all times, and “are not traded on margin.” CFTC portal copy. A commodities-law swap label is evidence. It is not, by itself, a conclusion that section 1256(b)(2)(B) applies.

Wagering — consequences if, and only if, that character is used

Topic 419 requires all gambling winnings to be reported, including winnings with no Form W-2G. Losses are deductible only if the taxpayer itemizes, and only with records. The topic page retrieved on 24 September 2026 does not mention the 90 percent limit. Section 165(d), as retrieved the same day, does:

(A) shall be equal to 90 percent of the amount of such losses during such taxable year, and (B) shall be allowed only to the extent of the gains from such transactions during such taxable year.

26 U.S.C. § 165(d)(1)

FTB dates that federal change to taxable years beginning after 31 December 2025. A 2025 federal wagering year is not automatically under the 90 percent rule. Do not apply 2026 law to 2025, or the reverse.

Section 165(d)(2) includes in “losses from wagering transactions” any deduction otherwise allowable that is incurred in carrying on a wagering transaction. That expansion is part of the federal statute. California’s nonconformity to it is on the California page.

Net investment income tax

3.8 percent of the lesser of net investment income or MAGI over $200,000 single or head of household, $250,000 married filing jointly, $125,000 married filing separately. Topic 559. Interest is in the general definition. Net gain from disposition of property can be. Wages and active-business income are not. Event-contract profit is not named. Do not add 3.8 percent by default.

Information-reporting threshold is not a tax threshold

For payments made after December 31, 2025, the base threshold under section 6041(a) is $2,000.

Rev. Proc. 2025-32, section 2.15

That amendment is about payments a trade or business must report. It does not say income under $2,000 is untaxed. It also does not say event-contract trades are section 6041 payments. Kalshi’s help page still describing a $600 credit threshold is a conflict, recorded on the flags page.
