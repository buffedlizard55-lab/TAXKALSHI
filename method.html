This desk exists so a person does not have to reconstruct the official pages from scratch. It does not exist so a script can invent the next answer. Extension is a sourced edit, checked by the validator.

Before any edit

  1. Read the standing prompt in README.md at the repository root before changing a conclusion. The prompt is the scope. Do not widen it into penalties, withholding planning, or a personalized return.
  2. Re-read every source you intend to rely on. A quote already in data/findings.json is a lead, not a permission to repeat it after the page has changed.
  3. If the page no longer contains the quote, mark the finding stale in the feed. Do not silently keep it.
  4. Run python3 scripts/validate_ledger.py. It checks IDs, HTTPS sources, quote length, and empty CPA seats. It does not check that a quote is true. That is the re-read.

How to add a finding

  1. Add the source to data/sources.json with publisher, HTTPS URL, and the date retrieved.
  2. Add the finding to data/findings.json. Required fields: id, status, topic, claim, quote, source_id, why_it_matters.
  3. The quote must be words from the page, not a paraphrase wearing quotation marks. A research note must say it is a research note.
  4. Status is one of settled, supported, open, flag, rejected. Do not use settled for character.
  5. Append a feed item in data/feed.json and a short row on the summary page if the conclusion moved.
  6. Update the HTML page that states the conclusion. The JSON ledger and the prose must not diverge.

How to admit a CPA

The prompt asks for a team of real, licensed public accountants who critique each other. The seats exist. They are empty because a name on a blog is not a license.

  1. Look up the person on the official board. California: search.dca.ca.gov, board of accountancy. Other states: that state’s board, not a directory site.
  2. Record license number, status, expiration, the board URL, and the date the record was seen. A screenshot path in the repo is better than a memory.
  3. Re-read the published position from the author’s own page. Quote it. Do not quote a journalist’s paraphrase as the CPA’s words unless the journalism page is the source and is labeled as journalism.
  4. Set the seat status to something other than empty only together with license_board_url and license_record_date. The validator rejects a filled license seat without those fields.
  5. A single California CPA does not close a question. Seat 2 has to be filled before the panel can claim an accountant consensus. Until then, the primary-text seats control.
  6. Do not imply the CPA reviewed this site. They did not, unless they did, in writing, and that writing is linked.

What not to automate

A weekly job that fetches IRS pages and rewrites the summary would manufacture confidence. The validator and a manual link check are the automation that fits. A conclusion changes only after a person, or a later research session, re-reads the page and edits the ledger.

GitHub Pages serves this site as static files. There is no account, no calculator, and no box for a Social Security number. That is intentional. The prompt said there should be no manual input to complete the research. The reader reviews links. The reader does not feed the site a return.

Limits that block a finished legal answer

  • The IRS has not ruled. No amount of critique invents a ruling.
  • This desk is not a CPA firm and cannot sign a return.
  • California license verification is a form on a state site. It was not completed for a named author in this pass.
  • Contract terms can differ by market. A sports contract and an inflation contract may not share a character. The engine says so. It does not have the rulebook exhibit for every ticker.
  • Tax years differ. 2025 Topic 409 breakpoints, 2026 Rev. Proc. 2025-32 brackets, and 2025 California schedules are labeled. Mixing them is an error.
