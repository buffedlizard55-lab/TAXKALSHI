Withholding

The scoped person is not subject to withholding. Kalshi’s tax-documentation article does not describe income-tax withholding on event-contract trading profits. An information return is a report. It is not tax withheld.

Two boundaries, so the sentence is not over-read:

  • Backup withholding can apply to certain reportable payments if a taxpayer identification number is missing or the IRS notifies the payer. Kalshi asks for a Social Security number for identity verification and for tax reporting on credits. The stated facts exclude that backup-withholding case. This page does not explain how to arrange it.
  • A contest or prize is not trading profit. This pass did not use contest-rule PDFs as a source for contract earnings. Do not assume a prize rule applies to a settled contract.

Paying on time, without computing a penalty

The prompt excludes penalties. The law still has payment rules. They are cited so a reader does not treat “no withholding” as “no payment until the return is filed.”

If you have a taxable capital gain, you may be required to make estimated tax payments.

IRS Topic 409

Generally, you do not have to make estimated tax payments if the total of your California withholdings is 90% of your required annual payment. Also, you do not have to make estimated tax payments if you will pay enough through withholding to keep the amount you owe with your tax return under $500 ($250 if married/registered domestic partner (RDP) filing separately). However, if you do not pay enough tax either through withholding or by making estimated tax payments, you may have an underpayment of estimated tax penalty.

2025 Form 540 booklet, important dates

The 2025 booklet lists 2026 California estimated-payment dates as 15 April 2026, 15 June 2026, 15 September 2026, and 15 January 2027. Those dates are for 2026 estimates, printed in the 2025 booklet. Confirm the date that applies to the year being paid before relying on a calendar in a research note.

This desk stops there. It does not compute the required annual payment, the federal safe harbor, or a penalty. Those calculations need the rest of the return, which this project does not have and does not ask the reader to type in.

Records worth keeping

Kalshi says the P&L is updated on the first of each month and that a mid-month look can miss the latest trades. Download the year after the January update, and keep the trade history beside it. Fees are inside Kalshi’s P&L figure. Whether a fee adjusts basis or is a separate expense follows the character analysis, which is open. Do not invent a fee rule.

Topic 419’s recordkeeping paragraph — diary, receipts, tickets — applies if the activity is wagering. It is not a universal Kalshi record rule. It is the rule that applies if that character is the one used.
